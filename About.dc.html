About

I am a web designer, front-end developer and software engineer, currently working as a technical lead.

That combination means fewer handoffs, fewer compromises between the design and what ships, and a team that has one person who can answer both sets of questions.

As a technical lead I set standards, review work, and keep the day-to-day moving — while staying close enough to the code and the pixels to still make things myself.
