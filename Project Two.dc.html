Makini Bush Camp identity

Year 2019
Role Logo design, brand identity
Deliverables Icon, wordmark, palette, type, applications

A logo and identity system for a bush camp. The mark is inspired by the camp's tents — the canvas silhouette drives the icon's geometry, which then sets the proportions for the wordmark lockup. The guidelines cover icon construction and clear space, the icon-with-wordmark lockup at four sizes, the colour palette, the Rajdhani type family across five weights, and brand applications.

Icon with wordmark

Two crossed tent silhouettes read as an M, with the poles left visible above the canvas line.
